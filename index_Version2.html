Plan your project

Choose colours and prepare surfaces well

Colour and surface condition both contribute to the final result. Think about the light, furnishings, and atmosphere you want in each room.

Choosing colours for your HDB home

Lighter shades such as white, cream, beige, and soft grey can help smaller flats feel open and airy. Soft earthy tones and neutral combinations can also create a warm, modern interior.

  • ✓Room size and natural lighting
  • ✓Existing furniture and interior decoration
  • ✓Your preferences and lifestyle
  • ✓The overall design of your flat
  • ✓The selected paint’s suitability and finish

Why surface preparation matters

Before painting begins, surfaces may need to be assessed and prepared. Depending on their condition, work may include cleaning, removing loose paint, filling minor holes, smoothing uneven areas, and protecting furniture and floors.

  • ✓Supports better paint adhesion
  • ✓Helps create consistent colour coverage
  • ✓Can improve the smoothness of the finish
  • ✓Helps reduce the visibility of minor imperfections

Is it time for a refresh?

When should you repaint your HDB flat?

There is no single repainting schedule for every home. The condition of your existing paint and your household’s needs can help determine when it is time to plan a refresh.

  • Visible paint peeling Peeling or flaking paint may need assessment and proper preparation before repainting.
  • Faded wall colours Light exposure and everyday use can gradually change the appearance of painted surfaces.
  • Stubborn stains and marks Persistent stains and scratches can make walls look untidy even after regular cleaning.
  • Home renovation Painting can help complete an updated interior design.
  • Moving into a new flat A fresh repaint can help personalise your home before arranging furniture and decorations.
  • Preparing to sell or rent A refreshed interior can help improve the presentation of your property.
